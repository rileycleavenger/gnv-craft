# The Standard at Gainesville

1360 W University Ave. Built 2017, 10 stories, 430 units / 1,200 beds.

## How it is built now
- **Footprint and height:** from the USGS 2018 LiDAR (building #261 in the bake). It is the long complex on the NW corner of 13th St & University Ave; OSM mislabels it "AC Hotel", which `tools/lidar/overrides.json` corrects. LiDAR height 38 m -> 10 levels.
- **Rooftop pools:** where the 2023 NAIP aerial photo shows pool water on the roof.
- **Facade:** from Mapillary street photos (2021): tan stone-veneer ground floor with cream recesses (Orangetheory Fitness in the corner storefront), red brick with tan panels and dark balcony rails above.
- **Interior:** generated. Double-loaded corridors with units on both sides, a lobby on the ground floor. The published plan names (Soho, Astoria, Brookhaven, Chelsea, Deerwood, Hounslow, Greenwich) are not individually placed: no public source gives which unit is where.
